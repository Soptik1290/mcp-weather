# Privacy Policy for Weatherly

**Last updated: February 2026**

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at Weatherly. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

## 1. Information we collect

When you use our mobile application, we collect the following types of information:

*   **Location Data:** We collect your precise or approximate location data to provide localized weather forecasts, astrological data, and ambient themes. This data is only processed temporarily (in real-time) on our backend servers to fetch weather from third-party APIs and is **not stored permanently** or tied to any identifiable user profile.
*   **Crash Logs & Diagnostics:** We collect crash logs and device diagnostics via Firebase Crashlytics to help us identify bugs, crashes, and improve the overall performance of the application.
*   **Purchase History & App IDs:** We use RevenueCat to process in-app subscriptions securely. RevenueCat generates an anonymous App Instance ID to track purchase history and active entitlements.

## 2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect or receive:
*   **To deliver services to you:** Specifically, fetching accurate weather data for your exact location.
*   **To manage user accounts and billing:** To process payments and unlock Premium features (AstroPack) without requiring a user login.
*   **To improve our App:** We use diagnostics and crash reports to fix bugs and improve user experience.

## 3. Will your information be shared with anyone?

We only share information with the following third parties strictly for the purpose of operating the application:
*   **Weather APIs:** Your coordinates are sent to third-party weather providers (e.g., Open-Meteo, OpenWeather) to fetch forecasts.
*   **Google Play Services & RevenueCat:** For processing payments and managing subscriptions.
*   **Firebase (Google):** For crash reporting and anonymous analytics.

We **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal information or location data to any third parties for marketing purposes.

## 4. Accounts and Data Deletion

Weatherly operates fully anonymously with no user accounts. The data collected (e.g., crash logs) is automatically deleted after 90 days. Because we do not collect personal identifiers like names or emails, there is no personal account to delete.

## 5. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this notice, you may contact the developer via the respective App Store contact channels or GitHub repository.
